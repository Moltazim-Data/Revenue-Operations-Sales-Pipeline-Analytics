# Metric definitions

- **Win rate:** won opportunities divided by closed opportunities. Open opportunities are excluded from the denominator.
- **Booked revenue proxy:** sum of opportunity amount where stage is Closed Won. This is a portfolio metric, not recognized accounting revenue.
- **Open pipeline value:** sum of amount for opportunities not marked closed. It is unweighted and is not a forecast.
- **Average won deal:** booked revenue proxy divided by won opportunity count.
- **Sales cycle:** days between creation and close; use won opportunities for the headline median unless otherwise stated.

All records and values are synthetic and exist for analytical demonstration only.
